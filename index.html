IMPOSSIBLE ICE CUBE 🧊

Drag the melting ice cube into the freezer before it turns into soup. Watch out—the freezer teleports away!

HOW TO PLAY 🧊

👆
Drag The Cube

Click/touch & drag the ice cube across the frosty floor. Notice the slippery momentum!

🚪💨
Outsmart The Freezer

The freezer panics and teleports when you get close. Exhaust its dodges or corner it!

❄️
Grab Power-Ups

Catch Freeze Clocks to immobilize the freezer or Ice Packs to restore melting time.

⏱️🔥
Beat The Meltdown

If your ice integrity drops to 0%, you melt into a puddle. Deliver it chilled!
